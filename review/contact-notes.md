# Contact revision

Head office is shown only in Contact. Footer retains branch offices and adds social icons using the REI links from the saved original website source.

Direct contact sits beside an enquiry form, separated by "or" (horizontal on mobile). The form requires name, email and message; company is optional. It opens a WhatsApp draft or a mailto draft using Email 1 (admin1@reisistem.id) or Email 2 (admin2@reisistem.id). Both addresses were decoded from the original source's Cloudflare email attributes. Sending remains a user action in the destination app; no backend delivery is claimed.

Checked at 1280, 820, 390 and 320px with no horizontal overflow. Required field validation and Email 2 selection were checked in the browser. No enquiry was sent. Build and lint passed.
