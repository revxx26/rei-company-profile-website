# Final interface review

Latest scope: dark mode is deferred and its control, storage handling and dark stylesheet rules are removed. Header consultation was replaced by an EN/ID language selector. Contact keeps the enquiry form on the right and a consultation popup button on the left. Head office appears only in Contact; footer keeps branch offices and social icons.

Additional glass styling is limited to service category navigation, homepage Explore services, language selector, floating consultation, and Contact buttons. Existing training/publication/form styles from earlier accepted revisions remain; the finalization-wide glass changes to header navigation, service rows, video cards, and other buttons were removed.

EN/ID translations use a React context and a local dictionary, including service details, regulatory summaries, training descriptions, article content, controls and form labels. Language is stored in localStorage, with an in-memory fallback. Native poster images and PDFs retain their original contents. Indonesian search terms are matched against translated service titles/scopes. Draft messages are localized and remain user-sent in WhatsApp/email.

Scroll entry animations use IntersectionObserver and the Web Animations API with distinct section treatments. Content remains visible before JavaScript; reduced motion skips/cancels effects. Article navigation still resets immediately rather than animating the route upward.

Validation: EN/ID switching, persistence across routes, Indonesian service search, service and consultation popup contents, Contact email selection, mobile menu, and absence of header consultation/dark mode were checked. Layout checked at 1280, 820, 390 and 320px. A transient horizontal overflow from scroll entry motion was fixed by clipping the main horizontal overflow. No enquiry was sent.
Final checks: production build, TypeScript compilation and ESLint passed. Browser console contained no application errors.
Follow-up revision: replaced native language select with a glass listbox matching the trigger. Keyboard arrows, Home/End, Escape, selection focus, outside click and focus exit are supported. Restored the original blue pill shape for Explore services with glass material; video and company-profile play circles use blue glass. Unselected service categories are plain rows with original dividers, while only the selected category receives the rounded blue glass background. Checked menu geometry at 320, 390 and 820px, EN/ID switching, keyboard navigation, selected category styling, and browser errors. Build and lint passed.

Popover menus render in the top layer for modal compatibility, clamp within viewport edges, reposition or close as the anchor leaves view, and support keyboard arrows, Home/End, typeahead, Enter, Escape, outside click and focus exit. Contact stores the topic in a hidden named input for FormData. Build and lint passed.
